# Backtracking

## Overview

Backtracking explores possible choices recursively and removes a choice when it cannot lead to a valid solution.

## General Pattern

1. Choose an option.
2. Explore the option.
3. Undo the choice.
4. Try another option.

## Problems

1. Subsets.
2. Permutations.
3. Combination Sum.
4. Letter Combinations of a Phone Number.
5. Generate Parentheses.
6. N-Queens.
7. Sudoku Solver.
8. Word Search.
9. Palindrome Partitioning.
10. Rat in a Maze.

## Important Concepts

- Recursion
- Decision tree
- Choice
- Constraint
- Backtrack
- Pruning

## Complexity

Backtracking solutions can have exponential time complexity because they may explore many possible combinations.
