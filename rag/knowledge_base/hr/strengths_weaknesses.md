# Strengths and Weaknesses

## Overview

These questions help interviewers understand a candidate's self-awareness and approach to personal development.

## Common Strength Questions

1. What are your strengths?
2. What technical skill are you strongest in?
3. What makes you different from other candidates?
4. What do teammates appreciate about you?
5. What is your strongest professional quality?

## Common Weakness Questions

1. What is your biggest weakness?
2. What skill are you currently improving?
3. Tell me about an area where you need improvement.
4. What feedback have you received?

## Good Weakness Structure

A useful answer should include:

1. Genuine development area
2. Awareness of the issue
3. Steps being taken to improve
4. Evidence of progress

## Important Guidelines

Candidates should avoid presenting a weakness that directly contradicts a core requirement of the role.

The focus should be on self-awareness and continuous improvement.
