# Project Interview

## Overview

Project questions evaluate whether a candidate understands the projects listed on their resume.

## Common Questions

1. Explain your project.
2. What problem does your project solve?
3. Why did you choose this project?
4. What technologies did you use?
5. What was your role?
6. What was the biggest challenge?
7. How did you solve that challenge?
8. What would you improve?
9. How did you test your project?
10. How would you scale the project?
11. What did you learn from the project?
12. What alternatives did you consider?

## Technical Follow-Up

Interviewers may ask about:

- Architecture
- Database
- APIs
- Algorithms
- Libraries
- Deployment
- Security
- Performance
- Design decisions

## Important Guidelines

Candidates should be able to explain every major technology and contribution mentioned in their project.

The candidate should clearly distinguish their own contribution from the work performed by teammates.
