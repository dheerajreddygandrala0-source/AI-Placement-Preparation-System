# Leadership

## Overview

Leadership questions evaluate initiative, responsibility, decision making, and the ability to help a team achieve its goals.

## Common Questions

1. Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership.
2. Have you ever led a project?
3. How do you motivate a team?
4. How do you handle disagreements within a team?
5. Tell me about a difficult decision you made.
6. How do you delegate tasks?
7. How do you handle responsibility for failure?
8. What does good leadership mean to you?

## Important Leadership Skills

- Communication
- Decision making
- Delegation
- Accountability
- Problem solving
- Conflict management
- Initiative

## Evaluation Focus

Strong answers should describe a specific situation and clearly explain the candidate's actions and results.
