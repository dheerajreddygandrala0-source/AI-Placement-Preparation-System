# Career Goals

## Overview

Career questions evaluate whether a candidate has thought about their professional direction and development.

## Common Questions

1. Where do you see yourself in five years?
2. What are your short-term goals?
3. What are your long-term goals?
4. Why do you want this role?
5. What skills do you want to develop?
6. What kind of work interests you?
7. Why did you choose this career?
8. What are you looking for in your first job?

## Important Areas

- Technical growth
- Professional development
- Learning
- Responsibility
- Career direction
- Contribution to the organization

## Good Answer Structure

A candidate can discuss:

1. Current skills
2. Skills they want to develop
3. Type of work they want to perform
4. Responsibilities they hope to take on
5. Long-term professional direction
