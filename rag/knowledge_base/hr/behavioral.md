# Behavioral Interview

## Overview

Behavioral interviews examine how candidates handled situations in the past.

## Common Topics

- Teamwork
- Communication
- Conflict resolution
- Problem solving
- Adaptability
- Responsibility
- Time management
- Failure
- Learning from mistakes

## Common Questions

1. Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.
2. Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
3. Describe a challenging project.
4. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate.
5. Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.
6. Describe a situation where you worked under pressure.
7. Tell me about a time you received critical feedback.
8. Describe a situation where you showed initiative.

## STAR Method

A useful structure for behavioral answers is STAR:

### Situation

Explain the context.

### Task

Explain your responsibility.

### Action

Explain what you personally did.

### Result

Explain the outcome and what you learned.
