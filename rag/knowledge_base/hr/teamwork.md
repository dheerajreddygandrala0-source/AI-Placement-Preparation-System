# Teamwork

## Overview

Teamwork questions evaluate communication, collaboration, responsibility, and the ability to work with others.

## Common Questions

1. Tell me about a team project.
2. What role do you usually take in a team?
3. How do you handle disagreements?
4. Tell me about a conflict with a teammate.
5. How do you communicate technical ideas?
6. How do you handle a teammate who is not contributing?
7. How do you divide work within a team?
8. How do you handle different opinions?

## Important Skills

- Communication
- Collaboration
- Active listening
- Responsibility
- Conflict resolution
- Respect for different perspectives
- Knowledge sharing

## Evaluation Focus

Interviewers may look for:

- Clear communication
- Ownership
- Cooperation
- Problem solving
- Professional behavior
