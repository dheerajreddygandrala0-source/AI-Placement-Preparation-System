# Self Introduction

## Overview

Self-introduction questions allow interviewers to understand a candidate's background, education, skills, experience, and career interests.

## Common Questions

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Walk me through your resume.
3. Tell me about your educational background.
4. What are your key technical skills?
5. What are your interests?
6. Why did you choose your field?
7. What are your career goals?
8. What makes you suitable for this role?

## Good Answer Structure

A candidate can structure the introduction as:

1. Current education or role
2. Relevant technical skills
3. Projects or experience
4. Achievements or interests
5. Career direction

## Important Guidelines

- Keep the introduction concise.
- Focus on relevant information.
- Connect skills and projects to the target role.
- Avoid unnecessarily personal information.
